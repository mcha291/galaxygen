## 0. What this build is for

Five builds are done; BUILD_V ran out of rows with its "done" out of reach (Audit VI, F-5-10) and with the owner's
yardstick unmoved: neither field frame is near its goal picture, and three model batches moved none materially nearer
(D263). S96 parted the distance into eight gaps (`READING_PICTURES.md` §3): four the **display's** (the owner's own
defaults of October hide what the model holds), one the **metric's**, three the **model's** (no connected dust lanes;
a bar 2.4 times under the goal's in light; NGC 4414's 0.93 kpc stellar layer). This build takes them in that order,
because a display choice costs minutes and moves no pin, and because what the model lacks cannot be judged through a
display that hides what it has.

## 1. The three rules of the order

1. **The display first, and the display is the owner's** (D227; the viewer's direction since 2026-10-01): a session
   builds the control the pass lacks and measures combinations for the owner to load; the owner tunes live and pastes;
   the paste becomes the template's default by T1's procedure. No session rules a display value.
2. **A goal picture is never a target** (C6). A display combination is offered with its measured statistics, never
   fitted to them; a model gap is closed only by a law with a source (D1), read by a reader before it is built, and
   the yardstick is read after, not steered by.
3. **One model batch**, as BUILD_V ran them: the pin lifted once, the subset mid-session, the runner's full suite once
   on the final head, the merge into `main` on the owner's word.

`get_usage` before every spawn. Opus builds, runs and reads; Fable gates, and analyses where judgement is the work.

## 4. What each session reads

stage's tests and the reader's file. Nothing else by default.
